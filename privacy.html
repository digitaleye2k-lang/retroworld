Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you use retroworld.eu.cc (“Retro World”, “we”, “us”), a free collection of browser games published by Digital EYE, and our Android app that packages the same games. We have written it to be plain and specific; if anything is unclear, use the contact button at the bottom.

1. The short version

  • You do not need an account to play, and we do not ask for your name, address or payment details.
  • Your game progress, scores, trophies and settings are kept on your own device (browser storage), not on our servers.
  • The site may show ads from Google AdSense (website) and Google AdMob (Android app). Google and its partners may use cookies or advertising identifiers for those ads. You can control this (section 5).

2. Information stored on your device

Games and the site use your browser’s local storage to remember things such as your Retro ID, profile preferences, trophies, best scores, game saves, control and sound settings, display mode and background choice. This information stays in your browser. Clearing your browser’s site data removes it. We do not receive it unless you choose to send a submission to us yourself by email.

3. Information collected automatically

  • Hosting logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in order to deliver the site and keep it secure. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.
  • Fonts. Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is sent to Google to serve the font files.
  • Arcade games. Games open inside Retro World load from the same site; they do not add extra tracking of their own.

4. Advertising and cookies

Retro World may display advertisements served by Google. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (and, in apps, advertising identifiers) to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites and apps. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to Retro World and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. Ad cookies help show relevant ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and measure ad performance and prevent fraud.
  • Google may use data to personalise ads if you have allowed it; otherwise ads are non-personalised and based on context such as the page content and your general location.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, ad personalisation and non-essential cookies are used only in line with the consent choices you give through the consent message shown on the site, which you can change at any time.
  • We do not sell your personal information and we do not place ad content on game controls or encourage you to click ads.

5. Your choices

  • Ad personalisation: manage or turn off personalised ads in Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of many third-party vendors via aboutads.info, youronlinechoices.eu or the NAI opt-out page.
  • Google’s own explanation: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google advertising technologies.
  • Browser controls: you can block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. The games still work without cookies, but saved progress is lost if you clear site data.
  • Android app: you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings → Privacy (or Google → Ads).

6. The Android app

The app contains the same games. It does not ask you to sign in. It may show banner ads through Google AdMob, which can collect device information and the advertising ID to serve and measure ads. The app requests internet access so ads and the in-game Arcade can load.

7. Children

Retro World is a general-audience arcade, but it is not directed at children under 13, and some games (for example Beat Up Mafia) contain cartoon violence and are rated 13+. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it. Where required, ads shown to users known to be children are limited to non-personalised ads.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California privacy laws) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to certain processing and to withdraw consent. Because we do not keep accounts or profiles on our servers, there is usually nothing to look up, but if you have emailed us we can delete that correspondence on request. Data held by Google or GitHub is governed by their own policies and tools.

9. Security and retention

Site traffic is delivered over HTTPS. Information you email us is kept only as long as needed to answer you. Data stored in your browser stays until you clear it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how Retro World handles data (for example by adding a new service), we will update this page and the date above.
