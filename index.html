QUICK GUIDE + WHAT WE WANT YOU TO TEST

QUICK GUIDE

  • Move the mouse to the edge of the map to scroll the camera.
  • Left-click ground to walk, or click a character to select them.
  • Double-left-click or right-click ground to run.
  • Right-click a character to crouch or stand.
  • Right-click a guard to quick-fire with the selected character.
  • Shift-drag to select several characters at once.
  • Use the character cards along the bottom to select your crew.
  • Use inventory cards for weapons, special tools and Whisper Rounds.
  • Crouch inside brush or use smoke to stay hidden from guards.

  • Ideas that make stealth, planning and multi-character tactics more satisfying.

:: ABOUT RETRO WORLD

MADE FOR RETRO GAME LOVERS

RETRO WORLD by Digital EYE was made for people who miss the feeling of loading up a simple game, hearing crunchy beeps, seeing bright pixels on a dark screen and instantly knowing it was time to play. We built this arcade for anyone who loves the spirit of old DOS games, 8-bit and 16-bit machines, coin-op cabinets and the days when games were quick to understand, hard to master and full of personality. It is also designed to be a perfect little companion for portable gaming — something you can open on a handheld, phone or tablet when you are on the go and want a quick trip back to those childhood gaming memories.

The idea is simple: open the site, pick a cartridge and have fun. The library is curated to avoid filler and near-duplicate games, so each cartridge earns its place. No complicated launcher, no online account required and no giant install. RETRO WORLD is a self-contained browser arcade with 58 curated games, music, sound effects, mobile controls, color themes, trophies and local player profiles.

ANDROID BETA: Want to help shape the next version of RETRO WORLD? Open the NEWS & UPDATES tab to try the Android Beta and send bug reports or feature suggestions directly through the built-in feedback form.

PORTABLE GAMING COMPANION

RETRO WORLD is built for desktop, laptop, Android phones, tablets and modern handheld gaming PCs such as Steam Deck-style devices. Standard gamepads can use the D-pad or left stick, A for action, Start to start/restart and B to close a game.

It is ideal for a train ride, holiday, sofa session or any moment when you want to relive a little of your childhood gaming on the go.

🎵

MUSIC + SFX

Choose RETRO 1–4 or load your own music. Games have their own retro sound effects, with separate MUSIC and SFX controls.

🎨

CHANGE THE LOOK

Use COLOR to switch between NEON, MATRIX, AMBER, PURPLE, RED and ICE. Use BG separately to choose STATIC, MATRIX RAIN, STARFIELD, CYBER GRID, PIXEL PARTICLES or CRT WAVES. Both choices are remembered locally.

🏆

PROFILES + TROPHIES

Create local player profiles and track plays, wins, best scores and recent activity. Daily Challenges award tokens, and progression rewards can unlock Premium Games. Every cartridge now includes Easy / Normal / Hard play plus escalating level progression; action games add faster pressure and boss-challenge rounds as levels rise. SKY FLAPPER requires 600 challenge tokens + 5 trophies; GALACTIC GOLF requires 900 tokens + 7 trophies; HELLBYTE '93 requires 1,500 tokens + 10 trophies.

💾

SAVE + LOAD

Your profile autosaves in the browser. Use EXPORT SAVE and IMPORT SAVE to move your local arcade profile between PC and Android.

:: QUICK START GUIDE

01 — PICK A GAMEBrowse the full game library above and press PLAY NOW on any cartridge.
02 — DESKTOP CONTROLSMost games use Arrow Keys or WASD. Space is usually fire, jump or action. Enter starts or restarts.
03 — MOBILE + HANDHELDPhones and tablets get on-screen controls. Compatible handhelds and gamepads can use the left stick/D-pad, A for action, Start to begin/restart, and B to close the current game.
04 — CLOSE A GAMEPress the X in the top-right of the game player. Keyboard users can also press Esc.
05 — CHOOSE MUSICUse BGM for RETRO 1–4, or + OWN for your own local audio file. MUSIC ON/OFF controls background music.
06 — CHANGE COLORSUse COLOR for the arcade palette and BG for a static or animated background. Mix any color theme with any background effect.
07 — CREATE A PLAYEROpen the trophy/profile button to create or load a local player and view your records and trophies.
08 — BACK UP YOUR SAVEUse EXPORT SAVE from the profile screen, then IMPORT SAVE on another device whenever you want to move your account.
LOCAL SAVE NOTE: Player profiles are stored in the browser on your device. The GLOBAL VIEWS counter is shared across visitors and counts page loads through an external counter service. If that service cannot be reached, the site temporarily shows a local device fallback instead. Clearing browser/site data, uninstalling the browser or using private/incognito mode may remove them. Use EXPORT SAVE to keep a portable backup.
PORTABLE CONTROLLER NOTE: RETRO WORLD uses the browser's standard Gamepad API. Most modern handheld gaming PCs and USB/Bluetooth controllers expose a standard layout, but exact button labels can vary by device and browser. If a particular handheld maps buttons differently, the touch controls and keyboard controls remain available as a fallback.
