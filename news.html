TROPHY ROOM+36 TROPHIES28 OCTOBER 2026

A brand-new Trophy Room: levels, streaks, weekly quests

Trophies now live in their own Trophy Room, separate from your profile. Level up through ten ranks, collect Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum trophies with live progress bars, hunt down secret trophies, keep a daily check-in streak (with Streak Freezes), finish four fresh weekly quests and play a new Game of the Day for bonus tokens. All five newest games now report to your profile too.

BEAT UP MAFIA 3.1+1 TROPHY25 OCTOBER 2026

Save game, weapon shops, snow and weapon icons

Beat Up Mafia now has a save game with CONTINUE on the title screen, AMMU-NATION weapon shops where you spend your mission cash, snowfall with much longer weather spells, weapon icons on the HUD and pickups, tougher cars, and fighter jet guns that now hit cars and people. Dying no longer ends the game: you wake up at the hospital with no cash and no weapons.

Beat Up Mafia now covers a map more than twice the size: a harbour with piers and boats to ride, Smuggler's Isle with a drug-boat mission, an airport with planes and helicopters, and an army base with tanks. Fourteen new trophies.
