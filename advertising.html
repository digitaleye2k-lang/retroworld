Advertising Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Retro World is free to play. To cover running costs we may show advertisements, served by Google AdSense on the website and Google AdMob in the Android app. This page explains how ads work here.

Who serves the ads

Ads are provided and delivered by Google and its advertising partners. We do not choose individual ads and we do not receive personal information about you from advertisers. Google’s ads must follow the Google Publisher Policies and the AdMob policies.

Our rules for ad placement

  • Ads are clearly separated from game content and, where the format allows, labelled as advertising.
  • We never place ads over game controls, menus or buttons, and we never ask or encourage you to click on ads.
  • We do not use pop-ups that block the game, auto-redirects, or fake “close” or “download” buttons.
  • In the Android app, banner ads appear outside active gameplay (for example on menus) so they do not get in the way of play.
  • Ads are not shown on pages that have no publisher content, such as error or navigation-only screens.

Cookies, personalisation and consent

Google and its partners may use cookies or advertising IDs to show ads based on earlier visits, to limit repeats and to measure results. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland this happens only according to the choices you make in the consent message. You can change personalisation at any time in Google Ads Settings or with the other options listed in our Privacy Policy.

Content standards

Retro World does not accept direct sponsorships for adult, gambling, political or misleading content. The games themselves are free arcade games and experimental projects; some contain cartoon violence and are marked 13+. We do not run real-money gambling or any game that pays out real prizes.

Reporting an ad

If you see an ad that is inappropriate, misleading or broken, tell us the page or game, roughly when it appeared and what it showed, and we will report it to Google and block it where possible. You can also use the “Report this ad” or “Why this ad?” options on the ad itself.

Affiliate and sponsored links

Retro World does not currently contain affiliate links or paid placements other than the advertising described here. If that changes, those links will be labelled.
