Beat Up Mafia

A top-down open-city crime game in the spirit of the classic 2D crime sandbox. Steal any car, shoot your way through mob territory, escape the police and climb through thirteen missions across land, sea and air to the final showdown with Don Corvino.

The story

Naples, 1986. The Ferrante family runs a little restaurant by the harbour until Don Corvino’s men demand it be signed over. Enzo Ferrante refuses, and by sunrise the restaurant and Enzo are gone. His son Nico Ferrante crosses the ocean with one suitcase, his father’s gold ring and a single name: Corvino. The Don has built an empire in Vice Row on the stolen Ferrante fortune, and Nico has come to take back what is his.

The story plays as a short comic-book intro the first time you press Start. You can skip it with Esc or the Skip button, and replay it any time from the Story button on the title screen. Beat the Don and an epilogue plays.

The big map

The map is more than twice the size of the original city and is now an island: sea runs along the west, north, south and east coasts. Downtown Vice Row and its suburbs sit in the west and centre. The harbour runs down the east side with five piers and boats to borrow. Out in the bay are Smuggler’s Isle, a lighthouse rock and a treasure cay. In the south-west the airport has a terminal, tower, hangars and a runway, and beside it a restricted army base keeps tanks, gunships and a stock of heavy weapons. South of the runway a suburb adds more streets, shops and parks.

Vehicles

  • Cars: sedans, sports cars, muscle cars, pickups, trucks, buses, jeeps and the police fleet.
  • Boats: speedboats, jet skis, cargo boats and armed gunboats. You can only leave a boat near land.
  • Tank: heavily armoured (it takes a lot to blow one up), with a turret that aims independently and a main cannon. Stealing one draws a heavy police response.
  • Helicopter: climbs straight up and lands anywhere flat, including rooftop helipads (you step out beside the building). It carries a minigun and rockets. Press F to land. Aircraft that descend to rooftop height over a building (other than a helipad) crash and explode, so keep your altitude up in the city.
  • Army gunship: an armoured helicopter with a minigun and rockets, parked in the military base.
  • Small plane: accelerate down the runway to take off, hold S to descend and land on flat ground.
  • Fighter jet and private jet: a single army fighter jet waits on the runway; Space fires its guns and Shift its rockets; a private jet sits in the hangar. Both need a long takeoff roll.

Weapon shops

AMMU-NATION shops sit against building walls, spread across the city, and are marked by a red awning and a gold ring. Walk into the ring on foot and press B to open the shop, then press the number shown next to an item (or click it) to buy ammunition or a weapon with the cash you earn from missions and pickups: SMG, shotgun, rocket launcher, grenades, molotovs, flamethrower, minigun, sniper rifle, body armour and medkits. Weapons are shown as icons on your HUD and on pickups, and small vehicles are now tougher, so they take more hits before breaking down. Pay'n'Spray garages (not open to police, military or aircraft) are also built into building walls and now cost money ($100, plus $100 per wanted star). Fighter jets, gunships and helicopters have aim assist and can strafe cars and people on the ground.

How it plays

You are Nico, a newcomer in Vice Row, a city of grid streets, parks, parking lots and a neon-lit casino owned by the Corvino family. Everything in the city is yours to use: walk up to any car and take it, run errands for cash, fight the mob in its own streets and keep an eye on your wanted level.

Wanted level

Crimes that a police officer can see raise your heat. Stars now build up more slowly and the response grows gradually. At one star a couple of patrol cars and officers on foot come after you, and at two a few more. At four stars a SWAT van, snipers and a police helicopter with a searchlight join in, and at five stars the army arrives too, with armed jeeps and soldiers on foot, though in smaller numbers than before. Break line of sight and stay hidden for a few seconds and your stars fade; a Pay’n’Spray garage clears them at once and repairs and repaints your car for a small fee.

Police response

Police do not react the instant you commit a crime. Small crimes build up quietly; after a few, and a few seconds’ delay, you get your first star. Once you are wanted, new crimes raise the level straight away.

Wasted and busted

There is no game over. If your health runs out you are wasted: you lose all your cash, your weapons and your wanted level, and wake up at the hospital with just your fists, so your next stop should be a shop. If a police officer holds you on foot for about a second and a half you are busted: you lose your weapons and 30% of your cash, but not a life.

Swimming

You can swim: if you end up in the water you paddle slowly (no shooting while swimming), so you can always make it back to land from an island without a boat.

Base defences

Tanks parked in the army base now turn their turrets and fire cannon shells at you when you trespass, so keep moving or stay out of their line of sight.

Car hits

Moving cars can hurt you on foot. Each hit costs health and counts toward five; the fifth hit in a row kills you. The count resets if you avoid cars for about 45 seconds.

Day, night and weather

The world runs on a seven-minute day: dusk, night, dawn, a bright afternoon and a golden hour. The weather changes on its own between clear, cloudy, rain, thunderstorms, fog and snow, with long calm spells in between; rain and snow make roads slippery and fog hides the distance. Light and shadow are simulated: buildings block light from street lamps, headlights and fires, and cast soft shadows with darker edges around their bases. After dark street lamps, headlights, runway lights, police lights, fire and explosions all light the scene. Inside a car the music switches to the Street Pressure track; press R to turn it off and go back to the main game track.

The thirteen missions

  1. Wheels: steal your first car.
  2. Payday: collect four cash bags hidden around the city.
  3. Soldiers: beat eight marked mobsters.
  4. Heat: reach a three-star wanted level, then lose the cops.
  5. Limo Hit: destroy two mob limousines and their escorts.
  6. Rocket Raid: find the rocket launcher, then blow up five mob cars outside the Corvino casino.
  7. Dock Deal: head to the harbour, steal a boat and pass four buoys.
  8. Drug Run: steal the cargo boat from Smuggler’s Isle and deliver it to the harbour while gunboats hunt you.
  9. Air Mail: take off and catch four falling cash bags.
  10. Sky Strike: steal a helicopter and destroy three fuel trucks on the island.
  11. Armoured Assault: steal a tank and flatten the mob cars outside the casino.
  12. Military Heist: break into the army base, steal three crates and deliver them to the harbour warehouse.
  13. The Don: destroy Don Corvino’s armoured limo, then finish him on foot.

Weapons: fists, pistol, SMG, shotgun, grenades and molotov cocktails are found around the city. The rocket launcher, flamethrower, minigun and sniper rifle are kept in the army base, which is restricted: entering it gives you a one-star wanted level and the army soldiers open fire. Watch for enemy snipers: a red laser line warns you a second before they fire. Vehicle weapons: tank cannon, helicopter minigun and rockets. Weapon crates, health, armour, cash and cop-bribe pickups are scattered around the city and drop from defeated enemies.

A better first run

  1. Get a car first. Cars are your best weapon and your best armour. Heavier vehicles such as trucks and buses shrug off crashes that would wreck a sports car.
  2. Learn the handbrake. A tap of Space at speed slides the rear around corners and breaks a chase. Sports cars are quick but fragile; the police are slower than your fastest steal.
  3. Hide, don’t just run. Cops need to see you. Turn behind buildings and your stars start to fade after a few seconds. Pay’n’Spray (green markers on the radar) clears them instantly.
  4. Don’t fight the mob in the open. The streets around the casino are crowded with armed soldiers. Pick them off from cover, grab armour first and keep moving.
  5. Save rockets for the right targets. Rockets destroy cars and helicopters quickly; use the pistol or SMG on people.

Devices, saves and known limits

Best score, difficulty, control scheme and the sound setting are saved in your browser’s local storage where available. Private browsing or clearing site data may reset them. Use Save game in the pause menu (or press V while paused); the game also saves after every mission and shop purchase, and Continue saved game on the title screen picks up at your current mission with your cash, weapons and trophies.

Beat Up Mafia plays with a keyboard (or keyboard and mouse, or a gamepad), and on phones and tablets it switches to on-screen touch controls: drag on the left to move or drive, drag on the right to aim and shoot, plus FIRE, ENTER, RUN, BRAKE and weapon buttons. Rotate to landscape for the best fit. It draws a large city and live lighting, so a recent browser with hardware acceleration is best. A separate game tab gives more room than the catalogue player, and fullscreen is browser-dependent.

The game doesn’t respond to my keys.

Click once inside the game so the browser gives it keyboard focus. The game pauses automatically when you switch tab or window; press P to resume.

I can’t hear anything.

Browsers only start audio after you click or press a key, so use the Start button. Check that sound is on (press M). Radio only plays while you are inside a vehicle.

Is it suitable for children?

The game contains cartoon violence: shooting, explosions and running over pedestrians, with no gore beyond small stylised splats. It is rated 13+ and not intended for younger players.
