BEAT UP MAFIA — OFFLINE EDITION

PLAY
1. Extract the ZIP (do not open the game from inside the ZIP preview).
2. Open Beat-Up-Mafia.html in your desktop web browser.
3. Click START GAME (or press Enter).

No installation, account, internet connection, or server required.
The HTML file contains the whole game and can be moved on its own.
All graphics, sounds and radio music are generated by the game itself.

CONTROLS (Classic)
Up / W          Walk forward  |  accelerate
Down / S        Walk back     |  brake and reverse
Left, Right / A, D   Turn     |  steer
Space           Fire / punch  |  handbrake
F or Enter      Enter / leave a vehicle
Q, E, 1-5       Change weapon
Shift           Sprint
H               Horn
R               Next radio station (in a car)
P / Esc         Pause
M               Sound on / off

CONTROLS (WASD + Mouse)
WASD move, mouse aim, click or Space to fire. Switch on the title screen or
the pause menu. A gamepad also works (sticks, triggers, A to enter a car).

STORY
You are Nico Ferrante, an immigrant from Naples whose family was ruined by
Don Corvino's mob. A comic-style intro plays on your first start (Esc skips it,
the STORY button on the title screen replays it). Beat the Don for the epilogue.

HOW IT WORKS
- Seven missions, from stealing your first car to the Don Corvino boss fight.
- Wanted level 1-5: patrol cars, SWAT, a helicopter at 4+ stars. Pay'n'Spray
  garages (green on the radar) clear your stars and repair your car.
- Three lives. WASTED costs a life; BUSTED costs weapons and 30% of cash.
- Best score, difficulty, control scheme and sound setting are saved in your browser.

CONTENT NOTE
Cartoon violence (shooting, explosions, running over pedestrians). 13+.
