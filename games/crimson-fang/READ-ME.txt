CRIMSON FANG — OFFLINE EDITION

PLAY
1. Extract the ZIP (do not open the game from inside the ZIP preview).
2. Open Crimson-Fang.html in your desktop web browser.
3. Click START GAME. Enable SOUND ON for arcade audio.

No installation, account, internet connection, or server required.
The HTML file contains the whole game and can be moved on its own.
Offline typography uses fonts available on your computer.

CONTROLS
WASD / arrow keys: Move
Shift (hold): Sprint
Space: Ninja leap
J (hold): Sword combos
K: Invincible dash
U (hold): Throw shuriken
L: Spirit burst (100 spirit)
P / Escape: Pause
Enter: Start / continue
Touch buttons are available on touch devices.

SPECIAL MOVES
1: Rising Dragon — unlock at 5 kills; costs 40 spirit
2: Shadow Clones — unlock at 15 kills; costs 65 spirit
3: Star Tempest — unlock at 30 kills; costs 100 spirit
Specials share a short cooldown. Spirit regenerates over time and from sword hits.

TEN SCROLLING STAGES
Advance right, fight enemies along the road, and defeat the boss in the final arena.
Stage Select lets you start at any of the ten stages.

SAVES
Best score and kill unlocks use your browser's local storage when available.
Offline saves are separate from the online game's saves. Use the same browser
and file location to retain them. Private browsing, storage restrictions, moving
the file, or clearing browser data may reset saves. Gameplay still works if
storage is unavailable.

VERSION

Includes sprint, leap, shuriken, special unlocks, and all ten scrolling stages.
